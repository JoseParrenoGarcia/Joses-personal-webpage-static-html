## Writing Style & Approach

- **Evidence-based**: Heavy use of real-world case studies from top tech companies
- **Practical frameworks**: SBI model, 3Ps system, competency matrices
- **Series-based deep dives**: Multi-part explorations of complex topics
- **Question-driven**: "Are you sure you want to become a DS Manager?"
- **Interactive**: Regular AMA (Ask Me Anything) sessions with audience
- **Template & resource provision**: 50+ Data Science templates, Notion systems

## Unique Perspectives

1. **Data Science in the AI Era**: Exploring how DS roles evolve when "everyone can build a model"
2. **Rigour vs Speed**: Defending scientific rigour in the GenAI era
3. **System Architecture > Prompt Engineering**: Future belongs to system builders
4. **Hybrid Future**: Practical examples of AI agents in ML workflows
5. **Value Beyond Code**: DS value lies before and after the model, not in model building itself
